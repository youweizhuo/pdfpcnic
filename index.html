pdfpcnic

pronounced “PDF picnic”

Present PDF slides with a pdfpc-style presenter view, in the browser.

Opens the presenter here and the audience window for the projector.

Your slides open in this browser; they aren't sent anywhere.

      Help

      Pace

      Under the slide
      A line that fills as you spend this slide's planned time, over all your visits to it, then turns the late colors. A backup slide's runs against its own length.
      By the clock
      Where the talk would end if you moved on now, the slides after this one as planned, against the target end: early or late. On a backup slide, you'd go on from the last planned slide you were on.
      Notes
      What you'd have said by now, at this slide's planned pace, dims character by character; ref paragraphs don't count (turn it off in Settings).
      The line under the next slide is the whole talk: time spent, then paler to where the talk would end if you moved on now, this slide's planned part in yellow over it. It's colored by the pace, as is the time at the clock's right, where the talk would end:
      on time, or a little early;
      late; deeper as it gets serious and very serious (by default 1 and 2 minutes; see Settings);
      well ahead: by default more than a minute early.
      Red line: the target end, duration, else where the plan ends; its time is in red over where the talk would end.

      Same keys as pdfpc. Click a slide to step on, right-click to step back; double-click the audience window for fullscreen. Click the clock to switch time displays, where the talk would end for how early or late.
