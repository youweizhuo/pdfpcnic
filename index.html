pdfpcnic

Present PDF slides with a pdfpc-style presenter view, in the browser.

Files stay on this computer; nothing is uploaded.

      Help

      Pace

      Under the slide
      A thin line that fills as you spend this slide's planned time, then turns the late colors; once seriously over, it says by how much.
      By the clock
      Where the talk would end if you moved on now, the slides after this one as planned, against the target end: early or late.
      The bar: the slides as planned, this one yellow. The line under it is time spent, colored like the early/late by the clock:
      on time, or a little early;
      late; deeper as it gets serious and very serious (by default 1 and 2 minutes; see Settings);
      well ahead: by default more than a minute early.
      Notes
      What you'd have said by now, at this slide's planned pace, dims character by character; ref paragraphs don't count (set how much in Settings).
      Red line: the target end, duration, else where the plan ends.

      Same keys as pdfpc. Click or right-click the audience window to step; double-click it for fullscreen.

      Pace colors

      Measure in

      Late always shows, in orange, deep orange and red; early only past its level, in blue. Shares are of the talk's length (its duration, else the planned total).

      Saved in this browser. While the laser is on, + and − size its dot instead of the notes.
