      Where you'll finish at this pace, against the target end: early or late. The levels can be changed in Settings.
      The bar's top row: the slides as planned, this one yellow. Under it, time spent:
      on time: it ends under the yellow block (or a little short of it);
      behind: it has run past the yellow block; deeper as it gets serious and very serious (by default 3% and 6% of the talk; see Settings);
      well ahead: by default more than 5% of the talk short of the yellow block.
      Red line: the target end, duration, else where the plan ends.
