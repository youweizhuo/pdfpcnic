# Code of conduct

pdfpcnic follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be kind and constructive, assume good faith, and leave out harassment of any kind.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, Youwei Zhuo, at youwei@pku.edu.cn. Reports are kept confidential.
