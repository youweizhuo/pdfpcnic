      Help

      Pace

      Slide
      This slide's planned window, and the time left in it; once past it, how far over, and before it, how early.
      Talk
      The talk's length (duration, else the planned total), and the time left of it. The bar is the whole talk:
      time spent, up to now, the white line;
      what's left of this slide's window;
      the slides to come, as long as planned. Where they end is where the talk will end;
      spare time before the end;
      past the end: slides that won't fit, or time over.

      Same keys as pdfpc. Click or right-click the audience window to step; double-click it for fullscreen.

      Settings

      Saved in this browser. While the laser is on, + and − size its dot instead of the notes.
